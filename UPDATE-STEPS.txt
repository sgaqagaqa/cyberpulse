CyberPulse web app: new design (4 steps, about 3 minutes)

Your saved stories from the old version are kept.

1. Unzip this file. Inside you will see a folder called "web".

2. On github.com open your "cyberpulse" repository.
   Click "Add file", then "Upload files".
   Drag the whole "web" folder onto the page and wait for the upload bar to finish.
   Click the green "Commit changes" button.
   (If your browser won't accept a folder: first click the "web" folder in your repository,
    then "Add file", "Upload files", and drag the files inside this "web" folder instead.)

3. Click the "Actions" tab, then "Build feed" on the left, then "Run workflow" (twice).
   Wait about a minute for the green tick. This step is what publishes the new design.

4. Wait 2 minutes, then open your link again.
   iPhone home-screen app: swipe it away from the app switcher and open it again
   (do this twice if you still see the old look).
   Mac: just reload the page.

What's new: a feed you can scroll like X or Instagram, tap the speech bubble on any story to write
your own thoughts, bookmark to save, share to send a story with your take, a My thoughts tab that
collects everything you wrote, search, topic tags, a "new stories" button, and light and dark mode.
Your saves and thoughts stay on the device you wrote them on. Use "Back up" to move them.
